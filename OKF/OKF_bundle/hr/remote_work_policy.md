# 1. Scope and Operating Model


NexaCore uses a role-based hybrid model rather than a universal remote entitlement. The approved work mode is stored in the HRIS and reviewed when employees change roles, teams or locations.


## 1.1 Work modes





| Mode | Default office days | Remote days | Typical eligibility |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Office | 5 | 0 | Lab, infrastructure and designated operational roles |

| Hybrid | 3 | 2 | Most engineering, product, finance and corporate roles |

| Remote | 0–1 | 4–5 | Approved roles with low physical dependency |





## 1.2 Core hours and availability


Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00 IST unless a roster states otherwise.

Remote employees must maintain a reliable connection during scheduled work hours and attend required meetings.

A remote arrangement does not change the employee's expected output, security responsibilities or response commitments.

Teams may establish fixed overlap windows where customer, release or support requirements justify them.


## 1.3 Work location


The employee's approved remote location is recorded at city/state level. Working from another city for more than 5 consecutive business days requires manager approval; cross-state working may require People Operations review.


# 2. Eligibility and Approval Workflow



## 2.1 Eligibility scorecard





| Criterion | Weight | Pass condition |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Role physical dependency | 30% | No mandatory daily physical dependency |

| Security/data sensitivity | 20% | Role can operate under remote security controls |

| Customer coverage | 15% | Customer commitments remain covered |

| Performance history | 20% | No active performance improvement plan |

| Team operating fit | 15% | Manager confirms coverage and collaboration |




An eligibility assessment is a management control, not an automatic entitlement. A role may be remote-capable while a specific employee is temporarily required to work from office for operational reasons.


## 2.2 Approval workflow


Employee submits the remote-work request in the HRIS.

Manager reviews role coverage, performance, collaboration and schedule impact within 3 business days.

Function head approval is required for permanent remote status and for exceptions longer than 30 days.

People Operations updates the HRIS after approval; email approval alone does not change the authoritative work-mode record.

IT/Security review is required when the proposed arrangement introduces unusual device, network or data-handling risk.


## 2.3 Temporary remote exception


A manager may approve up to 10 business days of temporary remote work for an employee whose standard mode is Office or Hybrid. More than 10 days requires function-head approval.


# 3. Equipment, Workspace and Connectivity



## 3.1 Standard equipment allocation





| Item | Standard issue | Replacement trigger |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Laptop | 1 managed device | Lifecycle / failure |

| External monitor | 1 for eligible roles | Failure / approved ergonomic need |

| Headset | 1 | Failure |

| Keyboard + mouse | 1 set | Failure / ergonomic need |

| Security key | 1 where role requires MFA hardware | Loss / failure |





## 3.2 Connectivity


Employees are expected to maintain a stable internet connection sufficient for video meetings and approved work systems.

For recurring outages, employees should notify their manager and use an approved office or backup location where feasible.

Company work must not be performed over untrusted public Wi-Fi without the approved VPN/security controls.

Personal hotspots may be used temporarily where approved, but confidential data must remain inside approved systems.


## 3.3 Home workspace


The employee should maintain a private work area where screens, documents and conversations cannot be viewed or overheard by unauthorized persons. Physical company records should not be stored in shared household spaces.


## 3.4 Asset incidents


Loss or suspected theft of a company device must be reported to IT/Security immediately and no later than 30 minutes after discovery where reasonably possible. Delayed reporting can increase security exposure.


# 4. Security Controls for Remote Work



## 4.1 Authentication


Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for corporate identity systems.

Employees must not share passwords, session tokens, recovery codes or security keys.

Screens must lock after the configured inactivity period; employees should manually lock before leaving the workspace.

Privileged administrative access must use the approved privileged-access workflow.


## 4.2 Data handling





| Data class | Remote handling | Storage rule |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Public | Allowed | Approved company systems |

| Internal | Allowed | Approved company systems only |

| Confidential | Controlled | Managed device + approved storage |

| Restricted | Case-specific | Additional controls / approval |





## 4.3 Meetings and calls


Confidential customer, HR, security or financial discussions must be conducted in a private environment. Recording is prohibited unless the meeting owner has confirmed the required notice/approval and the recording is stored in an approved system.


## 4.4 Printing


Printing confidential or restricted information at home should be avoided. If business necessity requires it, documents must be secured and destroyed using an approved method when no longer required.


# 5. Productivity, Collaboration and Travel



## 5.1 Collaboration expectations


Employees should keep calendars accurate and mark availability for core collaboration hours.

Project status should be updated at the cadence defined by the team, typically at least twice per week.

A missed meeting due to connectivity or other remote-work disruption should be communicated through the team channel.

Remote status cannot be used as a reason to defer customer-critical or release-critical work without manager agreement.


## 5.2 Travel while working remotely


Working while travelling is permitted only when the destination is within an approved jurisdiction and the employee can maintain security and work-hour obligations. International work from outside India requires prior People Operations and tax/legal review.


## 5.3 Remote-work performance indicators





| Indicator | Target / control |

| --- | --- |

| Core-hours attendance | ≥ 90% of scheduled core-hour obligations, excluding approved leave |

| Required meeting attendance | As defined by team; customer meetings are mandatory unless delegated |

| Status updates | Minimum 2 per week for active project assignments |

| Security training | 100% completion by assigned deadline |

## 6.3 Review and revocation


Remote eligibility may be reviewed after a material role change, sustained collaboration failure, security incident, customer coverage issue or organizational change. Any revocation should be documented with the reason, effective date and transition plan.
