# **NexaCore Benefits Policy**

### **Version 1.8 | India Employee Benefits**

**Owner:** People Operations **Effective:** 01 Aug 2026 **Review:** 31 Jul 2027

## **1. Benefits Program Overview**

Effective 01 Aug 2026. The program below is synthetic company data designed for private RAG testing.

|**Benefit**|**Coverage / value**|**Eligibility**|**Enrollment timing**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Medical insurance|Employee + declared dependents; INR 5 lakh base|Full-time employees|Within 30 days of joining|
|Top-up medical|INR 10 lakh optional top-up|Full-time employees|Annual enrollment|
|Life cover|3 × annual fixed pay; cap INR 50 lakh|Full-time employees|Automatic|
|Accident cover|INR 25 lakh|Employees + approved contractors|Automatic|
|Meal allowance|INR 2,200/month|Eligible India employees|Payroll|
|Learning wallet|INR 30,000/FY|L2+ and confirmed employees|Manager-approved|
|Wellness allowance|INR 6,000/FY|Confirmed employees|Reimbursement|


### **1.1 Eligibility principles**

- Benefits eligibility is determined by employment status, location and the specific benefit rule.

- Contractors do not receive employee insurance unless their contract explicitly includes an approved coverage arrangement.

- Dependent additions require documentary evidence and must be completed during the defined enrollment window except qualifying life events.

- Benefits do not create a separate employment entitlement beyond the written plan terms.

### **1.2 Annual benefit cycle**

The standard plan year runs 01 April–31 March. Annual enrollment normally opens for 10 calendar days in March. Employees who do not make an election during the window retain the default medical plan and automatic life/accident coverage where applicable.

## **2. Medical and Insurance Administration**

### **2.1 Medical plan structure**

|**Tier**|**Base cover**|**Room limit**|**Co-pay**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Employee only|INR 5,00,000|Single private room|10% for selected non-network claims|
|Employee + spouse|INR 5,00,000|Single private room|10% for selected non-network claims|
|Family|INR 5,00,000|Single private room|10% for selected non-network claims|
|Senior dependent add-on|Separate declared limit|As plan schedule|As insurer schedule|


### **2.2 Claims process**

- Cashless hospitalization should use an insurer-network facility where available.

- For reimbursement, claim documents should normally be submitted within 30 calendar days of discharge.

- Incomplete claims may be returned for clarification; the original submission date is retained in the claims record.

- People Operations can assist with eligibility and insurer escalation but does not determine medical admissibility.

### **2.3 Life and accident coverage**

Life coverage is calculated as three times annual fixed pay, capped at INR 50 lakh. Accident coverage is INR 25 lakh. Coverage changes following compensation changes are reflected at the next insurer file refresh unless the plan administrator requires an immediate update.

### **2.4 Nominee management**

Employees are expected to maintain nominee information in the benefits portal. A nominee record is not the same as an employee's dependent record; each is maintained for a different administrative purpose.

## **3. Learning, Wellness and Professional Development**

### **3.1 Learning wallet**

|**Expense type**|**Annual cap**|**Approval**|**Evidence**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Certification exam|INR 20,000|Manager|Invoice + result/attempt proof|
|Technical course|INR 30,000|Manager|Invoice + completion proof|
|Conference|INR 25,000|Manager + function head|Registration + attendance|
|Books / technical material|INR 8,000|Manager|Invoice|
|Language / communication|INR 12,000|Manager|Invoice + completion|


### **3.2 Wellness allowance**

- Annual allowance: INR 6,000 per confirmed employee.

- Eligible categories include fitness membership, structured wellness programs and approved ergonomic assessments.

- Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the transaction date.

- The allowance is not convertible to cash and unused balance does not roll forward.

### **3.3 Development planning**

Managers should link learning spend to a role capability, upcoming project need or documented development goal. For L4+ employees, learning requests above INR 20,000 should identify the expected business outcome or capability gap.

### **3.4 Approval examples**

Example: an L3 engineer requests INR 18,500 for an approved cloud certification. The manager can approve if budget remains. An INR 28,000 conference registration requires manager and function-head approval. A request submitted after the employee's exit date is not eligible.

## **4. Family, Leave-Linked and Employee Assistance Benefits**

### **4.1 Parental and family support**

- Eligible employees may access statutory and company-provided parental benefits subject to applicable law and plan documentation.

- Benefit administration uses the HRIS employment record and approved supporting documents.

- A qualifying life event such as marriage, birth, adoption or loss of dependent may create a special enrollment window.

- Special enrollment requests should normally be raised within 30 calendar days of the event.

### **4.2 Employee assistance program**

The company funds up to 6 confidential counselling sessions per employee per benefit year through the approved provider. Usage details are not shared with line managers. Administrative reporting is limited to aggregate utilization metrics and invoice reconciliation.

### **4.3 Caregiver support**

|**Support**|**Annual entitlement**|**Use case**|
|---|---|---|
|Caregiver flexibility|Up to 10 working days|Temporary care responsibility|
|Emergency family leave|Up to 3 working days|Urgent family event|
|Ergonomic assessment|1 assessment/FY|Workstation or work setup|
|Dependent documentation support|Case-based|Insurance / statutory administration|


### **4.4 Privacy**

Benefits data may include health and dependent information. Access is restricted to People Operations benefits administrators and approved vendors. Managers receive only the information needed to administer leave or workplace accommodations.

## **5. Allowances, Reimbursements and Tax Treatment**

### **5.1 Monthly allowances**

|**Allowance**|**Monthly value**|**Payroll treatment**|**Notes**|
|---|---|---|---|
|Meal|INR 2,200|As applicable|Processed with monthly payroll|
|Internet|INR 1,500|As applicable|Remote-eligible roles|
|Mobile|INR 800|As applicable|Approved customer/support roles|
|Learning|Annual pool|Reimbursement|Not monthly cash|
|Wellness|Annual pool|Reimbursement|No rollover|


### **5.2 Submission deadlines**

- Monthly payroll-impacting benefit declarations should reach HR by the published payroll cutoff, normally the 18th calendar day.

- Reimbursement claims should be submitted within 45 days unless the specific plan states a shorter period.

- Claims with missing invoices, altered documents or unclear merchant details may be rejected or returned for clarification.

- Employees are responsible for retaining original documents where tax or statutory rules require them.

### **5.3 Benefit changes during employment**

Promotion, transfer, location change or employment-status change can alter benefit eligibility. People Operations reviews eligibility during the HRIS change workflow. A benefit should not be assumed to continue solely because it was available before a transfer.
