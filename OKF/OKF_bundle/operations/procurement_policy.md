# **NexaCore Procurement Policy**

### **Version 3.8 | Sourcing, Purchasing and Supplier Controls**

**Owner:** Operations & Procurement **Effective:** 01 Aug 2026 **Review:** 31 Jul 2027

## **1. Procurement Governance and Scope**

This policy governs the sourcing, purchasing, approval and receipt of goods and services for NexaCore. It is synthetic private-company data created for RAG evaluation and is not sourced from a real organization.

### **1.1 Procurement principles**

- Business need must be documented before material purchases.

- Competitive sourcing is expected for addressable spend unless a documented exception applies.

- Approvals must occur before commitment to a supplier.

- Employees must not create supplier commitments outside their authority.

- Supplier selection should consider price, quality, security, continuity, delivery and total cost of ownership.

### **1.2 Purchase thresholds**

|**Spend value**|**Required route**|**Minimum sourcing expectation**|
|---|---|---|
|≤INR 25,000|Manager approval|Reasonable market check|
|25,001–2,00,000|Purchase request + budget owner|2 comparable quotes where practical|
|2,00,001–10,00,000|Procurement review|3 quotes / documented exception|
|> INR 10,00,000|Procurement + Finance|Formal sourcing event or approved sole source|


### **1.3 Prohibited practices**

Employees may not split purchase orders, accept undisclosed supplier incentives, approve their own procurement request, or create commitments outside approved contracts.

## **2. Sourcing and Supplier Selection**

### **2.1 Sourcing stages**

- Requirement definition

- Supplier discovery

- RFI/RFQ/RFP where appropriate

- Commercial comparison

- Security/legal review where applicable

- Negotiation

- Approval

- Purchase order / contract

- Supplier onboarding

### **2.2 Evaluation model**

|**Criterion**|**Standard weight**|
|---|---|
|Commercial value|30%|
|Quality / technical fit|25%|
|Delivery / service capability|15%|
|Security / compliance|15%|
|Financial / continuity risk|10%|
|Sustainability / other factors|5%|


### **2.3 Sole-source exception**

Sole-source procurement requires written justification covering uniqueness, alternatives considered, commercial reasonableness and business impact. Purchases above INR 10,00,000 require Procurement Head approval for a sole-source exception.

## **3. Purchase Orders, Contracts and Receiving**

### **3.1 Purchase order rules**

- A PO is required for purchases covered by the procurement workflow unless an approved exception applies.

- Supplier invoices should reference the PO number.

- PO quantity and value should be monitored against receipts and invoices.

- PO changes above 10% of original value require approval from the original budget authority or higher.

### **3.2 Three-way matching**

|**Record**|**Purpose**|
|---|---|
|Purchase Order|Authorized commercial commitment|
|Goods Receipt / Service Acceptance|Confirms delivery or completion|
|Supplier Invoice|Amount requested for payment|
|Finance match|Validates PO + receipt + invoice consistency|


### **3.3 Service acceptance**

For professional or recurring services, the business owner confirms delivery against the statement of work before Finance releases payment.

## **4. Supplier Risk, Security and Compliance**

### **4.1 Risk tiers**

|**Tier**|**Typical supplier**|**Review**|
|---|---|---|
|Critical|Core infrastructure / critical customer dependency|Annual + continuous monitoring|
|High|Sensitive data or material operational dependency|Annual|
|Medium|Important business service|Every 18 months|
|Low|Commodity / low-impact supplier|Onboarding + periodic refresh|


### **4.2 Security review triggers**

- Supplier accesses Confidential or Restricted data.

- Supplier connects to production systems.

- Supplier provides identity/security services.

- Supplier hosts customer-impacting workloads.

- Supplier subcontracts material processing.

### **4.3 Insurance and legal**

Material suppliers may require confidentiality, security, service-level, data-processing, liability and business-continuity clauses depending on risk.

## **5. Supplier Performance and Payment Controls**

### **5.1 Performance measures**

|**Metric**|**Target**|
|---|---|
|On-time delivery|≥95%|
|Invoice accuracy|≥98%|
|Critical SLA compliance|≥99%|
|Corrective-action closure|≤30 days for high issues|
|Supplier response to critical incident|≤60 minutes|


### **5.2 Payment terms**

- Standard payment terms target Net 30 unless contract terms justify another schedule.

- Advance payments require additional approval and contract safeguards.

- Disputed invoices should be placed on hold for the disputed portion rather than delaying unrelated valid invoices.

### **5.3 Supplier remediation**

Suppliers with repeated performance failures may receive a corrective-action plan, increased monitoring, commercial penalty, volume reduction or termination recommendation.
