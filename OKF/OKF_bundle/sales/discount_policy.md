# 1. Discounting Principles


Discounts are commercial tools, not default pricing. Sales must establish business justification and protect minimum economics before requesting a concession.


## 1.1 Standard authority bands





| Discount | Account Executive | Sales Manager | VP Sales | CFO Delegate |

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 0–5% | May approve | Visibility | — |  |

| >5–10% | Request | Approve | Visibility | — |

| >10–15% | Request | Recommend | Approve | — |

| >15–20% | Request | Recommend | Approve | Finance review |

| >20% | Request | Recommend | Approve | CFO delegate |





## 1.2 Prohibited behavior


Splitting an order to avoid a discount threshold.

Backdating an approval.

Describing a discount as a temporary exception when it is intended as recurring pricing.

Offering unapproved free services or implementation credits.

Using a personal promise to compensate for a commercial concession.


# 2. Discount Eligibility and Justification



## 2.1 Acceptable justification


Multi-year commitment

Material expansion volume

Competitive displacement

Strategic reference account

Pre-approved segment promotion

Contract consolidation


## 2.2 Required evidence





| Discount band | Required evidence |

| --- | --- |

| ≤5% | Opportunity value + business rationale |

| >5–10% | Competitive/volume rationale + manager approval |

| >10–15% | Commercial comparison + margin impact |

| >15–20% | Executive rationale + Finance review |

| >20% | Strategic justification + CFO delegate review |





## 2.3 Margin protection


Discount approval does not override minimum gross-margin requirements. Finance may reject a discount even when the approval level is otherwise satisfied if the proposed economics fall below the product or deal floor.


# 3. Multi-Year and Renewal Discounts



## 3.1 Contract duration





| Term | Maximum standard discount | Conditions |

| --- | --- | --- |

| 1 year | 10% | Standard pricing guardrails |

| 2 years | 15% | Annual payment preferred |

| 3 years | 20% | Executive approval |

| 4+ years | Case-by-case | CFO delegate + Finance |





## 3.2 Renewal rules


Renewal discounts must be evaluated against current contract value, usage and expansion potential.

A renewal discount cannot be assumed from the prior year's concession.

Expansion and renewal discounts should be separated in the commercial proposal.

Credits for service issues require Customer Success and Finance validation.


## 3.3 Price protection


Price-protection clauses extending beyond 12 months require Finance review because they affect future price realization and forecast assumptions.


# 4. Bundles, Credits and Non-Cash Concessions



## 4.1 Non-price concessions





| Concession | Standard limit | Approval |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Free implementation days | Up to 2 days | Sales Manager |

| Training seats | Up to 10 | Sales Manager |

| Premium support trial | 30 days | VP Sales |

| Service credit | ≤5% annual contract value | Finance + CS |

| Custom development | Case-by-case | Product + Engineering + Finance |





## 4.2 Valuation


Non-cash concessions must be recorded at their estimated internal cost or list value, whichever is required by Finance's commercial model. Sales must not hide concession value by presenting it as an unrelated free item.


## 4.3 Free pilots


A standard proof-of-value should not exceed 30 calendar days without documented extension approval. Any production use during a free period must have explicit data, security and support ownership.


# 5. Approval Workflow and Audit



## 5.1 Workflow


Create commercial request in CRM.

Attach pricing proposal and justification.

Manager reviews economics and customer context.

Finance reviews margin where required.

Authorized approver accepts/rejects.

Revenue Operations records final approved discount before quote issuance.


## 5.2 SLA targets





| Request type | Target review |

| --- | --- |

| ≤10% discount | 4 business hours |

| 10–20% | 1 business day |

| >20% | 2 business days |

| Quarter-end exception | Same business day where feasible |





## 5.3 Audit controls


Revenue Operations samples approved discounts monthly. Any discount without an approval record is treated as an exception and may be excluded from commissionable bookings until resolved.
